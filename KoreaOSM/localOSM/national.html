Speed model and data limits

Bidirectional Dijkstra minimizes distance or travel time. Directional OSM speeds take priority. Route details show applied speeds and estimates.

Fallback speeds (km/h): motorway 80–100, motorway links 40, trunk 60, primary roads 50, residential 30, service roads 20. These are model estimates. Walking defaults to 4.5 and cycling to 15.

Lane and conditional speeds use the lowest parsed value throughout. Time and weather conditions are not evaluated. Unparsed tags are flagged.

Traffic, signals, turn restrictions, barriers, conditional access and ferries are not modeled. Mixed road mode excludes transfer costs.
